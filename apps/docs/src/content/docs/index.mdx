## A payment, as three links

A private payment hides its amount and memo onchain. These links are how the people involved share exactly what they need to.

**1. The shop asks to be paid.** A request carries the amount and an order number. The wallet puts the order number in the encrypted memo, never onchain in the clear.

```text wrap
ringpay:GNhaRndM8JGXUhsfgWXGR7XdcWnioBbuZueyaJ2zsYsD?amount=0.0125&order=1042&label=Corner%20Caf%C3%A9&cluster=devnet
```

**2. The customer keeps a receipt.** It opens that one transaction, so the shop, an accountant or a dispute resolver can check the amount against the chain.

```text wrap
ringrcpt1:AQCiZaSPDMA5O5BmcOeXkc8SElljb9_xs39R0bheudl26NgAhes63HXT_v9f5A18lQokEad9eW…
```

**3. A payee proves their own output.** Paid in a batch with others, an employee proves their pay to a landlord without revealing anyone else's.

```text wrap
ringproof1:AQD4gzzk5kJ1DCpQ2E1SH1R_7JQit20Dgn1PYYWzOdtVRt0I6nKDatsk6-W07NOzGqPoZC10y54q…
```

Paste either of the last two into the [verifier](https://lopeselio.github.io/ringstack/verify/) to check it. It runs in your browser and needs no account or keys.

## What's here

- [**ringd**](tools/ringd/): A self-hosted daemon that watches a wallet with read-only keys and sends signed webhooks when payments arrive, memo included. It can also merge small payments without the spending key.
- [**Private payroll**](tools/payroll/): A CLI that pays a CSV of people in batches of up to 15, resumes safely after a crash, and produces an audit bundle for the accountant.
- [**Agent payments**](tools/paywall/): An HTTP 402 paywall and an MCP wallet that let AI agents pay APIs privately, within spend limits.
- [**Leak analyzer**](tools/leakscan/): Scores how linkable the pool's public deposits and withdrawals are, warns before a withdrawal that would link back to you, and publishes a [daily aggregate report](https://lopeselio.github.io/ringstack/leaks/).
- [**Derivation guard**](tools/derivation-guard/): Refuses a website's request for the wallet signature that unlocks your private payments, unless you confirm. A detector wallets can embed, plus a [browser extension](tools/derivation-guard-extension/).
- [**Verifier page**](tools/verifier/): A static page that checks receipts and payment proofs in the browser, with no keys and no account.
- [**Checkout demo**](tools/checkout-demo/): A Next.js shop and NestJS API that take a private payment, hear about it from ringd, and mark the order paid.
- [**@ringstack/core**](overview/#packages): The TypeScript library underneath: wallet sessions, memos, payment requests, receipts and proofs.

Before relying on any of it, read the [privacy limits](guides/privacy/): what stays hidden, and what does not.
